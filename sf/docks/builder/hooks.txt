Hooks.

The grappling hook is a traversal tool that throws a line, catches onto something across the map, and pulls you to it, then lets you climb along the line to reach places you could not walk to. It is thrown with the hook keys (f to throw forward and v to throw backward by default) and works on 2d and 3d maps, but not on topdown ones.

Throwing and catching.

When you throw, the hook flies out in its direction until it reaches something it can grab or runs out of reach. On a 3d map, throwing forward sends the hook straight up and throwing backward sends it straight down; on a 2d map the two throws send it forward and backward relative to the way you are facing. If the line reaches something it can anchor to it catches, plays a catch sound, and pulls you across to the spot it was thrown to, leaving you hanging on the line. If it runs out of reach without finding anything it drops with a miss sound and nothing happens.

What the hook can grab.

The hook anchors onto walls, staircases, solid ground and platforms, vanishing platforms while they are present, and moving platforms. It cannot grab open air, hazards, or an empty gap. If the thing you are hooked to is destroyed, or a vanishing platform disappears while you are on it, the hook automatically lets go and drops you.

Climbing the line.

Once you are hooked, your movement keys pull you along the line toward the anchor instead of walking you around. You travel tile by tile until you reach the anchor's edge, where you step onto it and the hook releases on its own. On a 3d map you climb up or down along the line's height; on a flat map you travel across it. If you are hooked to a moving platform, you ride along with it as it moves.

Letting go.

Press the throw key again, or move in a direction that is not along the line, to release the hook early and drop where you are. Jumping also lets go. Releasing plays a release sound.

Sounds.

The hook's throw, catch, miss, release, and climbing sounds all come from your character's own map sound set, so different characters can carry differently themed hooks.

The arena.

The grappling hook is turned off inside the arcade arena, so its keys do nothing there.
