Builder galleries.

The sound gallery is a place to listen to the sounds that come with the builder's elements without opening each element's own form. It is a browsing tool only — nothing you do in it changes your map or places anything. You are just hearing what a platform, a door, a hazard, or any other element sounds like, so you can pick a theme you like before you build with it.

Opening the gallery.

Open the build menu and choose gallery. The gallery entry sits on every tab of the build menu, just before the template entry, so you can reach it no matter which category you are on.

Finding a sound.

The gallery is laid out the same way the builder's sounds are organized. First you choose a category — audio, construction, interaction, kombat, transitions, transportation, traps, or zones. That opens a set of tabs, one for each kind of element in the category, and each tab lists the sound themes available for that element. Press tab to move between the element tabs, and use the arrow keys to move through the themes in the focused tab. Each theme's name is spoken as you land on it.

Playing sounds.

Press space to play the highlighted theme's main sound. Hold control and press a letter to play its extra sounds; which letters do what depends on the element, and they are the same keys that element's build form uses to audition the same clips. For example, a platform plays its footstep on space, its landing on control L, and its hard landing on control H, while a door plays its ambient loop on space and its open, close, and other clips on other control letters. If a theme is missing a particular sound, the gallery tells you so instead of playing nothing.

Bike surfaces.

Bikes carry a second set of sounds for the terrain they ride over, reached from the same bike theme without leaving it. Hold control and press the apostrophe key to step to the next surface, or hold control and press the semicolon key for the previous one; each announces its name. Then, for the surface you are on, hold control and press space, K, or J to hear it rolled over, landed on, or fallen onto. Pressing an arrow key to move through the bike list returns you to the first surface.

Leaving the gallery.

Press escape to step back a level — from the themes to the category list, and from the category list out of the gallery.
